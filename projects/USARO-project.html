Design Project Overview

For my senior design project at the University of Colorado Boulder, I worked as Logistics Manager for a team of 9 in collaboration with the United States Army Research Office.

Image source: University of Colorado Boulder Paul M. Rady Mechanical Engineering

The image seen to the left depicts the scaled model of an autonomous ground-based device, which was developed to enhance warfighter protection, supply, and evacuation. In my role as Logistics Manager, I led integration of the two prior year's projects into one fully functional device while adding new autonomous capabilities. I worked directly with the government customer to define requirements, coordinated weekly meetings, and co-authored reports and presentations to demonstrate that our final design met or exceeded all performance objectives.

This project strengthened my understanding of system integration and reinforced the importance of a "trust but verify" mindset, especially when inheriting work from previous contributers. It gave me a realistic introduction to how complex projects operate in industry and helped me build confidence navigating a real career in engineering.
