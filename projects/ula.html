Design Project Overview

I started my time at ULA as a Mechanical Design Engineer on the Vulcan Development team, working with the Transportation and Handling group to create transport fixtures, access platforms, and handling hardware to assist with processing of the rocket at the launch sites.

The image to the left shows a payload fairing assembly being processed on a large support fixture (the white structure on the ground). Although I was not the lead designer of the fixture pictured, I designed a similar piece of ground support equipment for a different application. The image provides context for the type of hardware and use cases I supported during this project.

This project gave me valuable experience with nearly every stage of the design process. I worked directly with launch operations personnel to define requirements and constraints, while brainstorming ways to improve operational efficiency. I took these inputs and used them to develop concept models in NX, and iterated my design based on inputs from end users, structural analysts, and senior engineers. After a formal review to approve the final design, I created engineering drawings that applied GD&T principles and released the engineering for procurement. Throughout the hardware build process, I gained experience coordinating with vendors, resolving manufacturing issues, supporting proof load testing, and managing schedule-driven challenges. The project required continuous collaboration across engineering, manufacturing, and operations teams, ultimately resulting in successful hardware deployment and essential lessons in both technical design and project execution.
