Design Project Overview

CleanRobotics was my first engineering internship, which I began the summer after my junior year of college. I initially joined as a Robotics/Mechanical Engineering Intern and was hired on part-time as a Mechanical Engineer while completing my senior year. As an early stage startup, CleanRobotics gave me the opportunity to wear many different hats and learn a broad range of skills that blended engineering, design, and user experience.

Image source: TechCrunch

This image shows CleanRobotics flagship product, TrashBot. TrashBot is an AI-powered smart bin that identifies and sorts waste into landfill, recycling, and compost streams. I fabricated and assembled 14 production units, using rapid prototyping and 3D printing to test and implement mechanical improvements that increased TrashBot's overall performance.

My most exciting contribution was a UX-centered redesign of the TrashBot exterior and user interface. This project allowed me to merge my interests in aesthetic design and human behavior with my technical background. I conducted market research, developed concept sketches, and iterated through multiple design directions in Solidworks to create high-fidelity product renderings. A major focus of this redesign was exploring gamification strategies to increase user engagement, reinforce educational content, and create a more intuitive and rewarding experience for the user.

Beyond the redesign effort, I supported testing, electronic manufacturing, and early steps toward scaling TrashBot for mass production with a vendor. The range of responsibilities I took on at CleanRobotics showed me how a product evolves from concept to production, and the UX and aesthetic redesign made it clear how much I enjoy merging visual appeal, user behavior, and engineering to create products that feel thoughtful and intuitive.
